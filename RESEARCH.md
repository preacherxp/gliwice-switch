# Gliwice scene references

Researched 9 November 2026 for the additions made that day: the plane trees, the Metrorower, the pigeons, the terrace guests and the sky. The landmarks themselves were drawn earlier and are not covered here; their links are in the README. The scene remains a **compressed panorama**, not a street view.

## What was added and why

| Element | Verified | Pixel-art features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **London planes on the Rynek** | The city replaced 8 small, ailing trees with **11 London planes** (*platan klonolistny*) planted in the ground on three sides of the square, beside low beds of shrubs and perennials, in works the city scheduled for 2022. [City announcement, 10 March 2022](https://gliwice.eu/aktualnosci/miasto/rynek-wypieknieje-bedzie-wiecej-zieleni), [planting report](https://gliwice.eu/aktualnosci/miasto/zielonegliwice-sadza-drzewa-na-rynku) | Slim pale stem, tall upright open crown that the façades show through, fresh yellow-green leaf. The existing stone-edged boxes stand in for the low beds. |
| **Metrorower** | The metropolitan hire bike (GZM with Nextbike) started in Gliwice on 25 February 2024; the city describes yellow-and-black fourth-generation bikes with a rear-wheel lock. [City launch article, 26 February 2024](https://gliwice.eu/aktualnosci/ekologia/metrorower-wystartowal) | Yellow step-through frame, yellow front basket, yellow panel over the rear wheel, black tyres, saddle and bars; upright rider; white front and red rear light after dark. |
| **Pigeons** | A flock on the Rynek paving and birds perched on the Neptune fountain's basin appear in local press photographs; the article names the old town as where they are most felt. [24gliwice, 20 February 2017](https://www.24gliwice.pl/wiadomosci/golebie-brudza-i-niewiele-mozna-z-tym-zrobic-wg-rozporzadzenia-ministra-srodowiska-sa-pod-ochrona/) | Blue-grey birds with darker heads, pecking on the open paving in front of the Ratusz; single birds loop up and back, the flock goes up on a fast flick of the lens and roosts at dusk. |
| **Terrace guests** | The city's own photograph of the Rynek shows people seated under cream canopies and families crossing the square. | Seated figures at the café tables by day and at the pub's courtyard tables in the evening; a cup is raised now and then. |

## Images actually opened

Used as drawing references only, not bundled; no reuse licence has been established.

- **Rynek, Ratusz and terraces:** [city photograph](https://gliwice.eu/sites/default/files/news/gallery/rynek_um.jpg), EXIF 9 August 2021, so before the planes were planted. Seated guests under a cream canopy, lantern posts, small flat-based cumulus in a clear sky, which is the cloud shape used here.
- **Young planes on the Rynek:** [planted tree and bed](https://gliwice.eu/sites/default/files/news/gallery/rynek1a.jpg) and [planting in progress](https://gliwice.eu/sites/default/files/news/gallery/rynek3.jpg), city photographs published with the planting report; capture date not stated. Thin stem, narrow open crown, rectangular stone-edged bed, benches.
- **Metrorower:** [bikes at a Gliwice station, M. Buksa / UM Gliwice](https://gliwice.eu/sites/default/files/news/gallery/Metrorower%20fot_MBuksa%20Z62_4287.jpg) and [a bike in side view, G. Ożga / UM Gliwice, EXIF 25 February 2024](https://gliwice.eu/sites/default/files/news/gallery/P2251981.jpg).
- **Pigeons:** [flock taking off on the square](https://www.24gliwice.pl/wiadomosci/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/go%C5%82%C4%99bie-rynek-810x456.jpg) and [birds on the fountain basin](https://www.24gliwice.pl/wiadomosci/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/go%C5%82%C4%99bie-fontanna-808x455.jpg), ŁG / 24gliwice.pl, 2017.

## Placement in logical pixels

- Planes keep the three existing tree positions (`x` 89, 236, 345); crowns span `y` 86–106.
- Pigeons stand at `x` 141–200, `y` 115–119, between the Ratusz steps, the second terrace and the fountain. Their flights stay within that span and below `y` 87, clear of every sign and of the walk lanes (`y` 130–134, heads from 121).
- Guests sit on the existing chairs; no furniture moved.
- The Metrorower keeps the old cyclist's lane (`y` 142) but rides right to left.
- Sky: the sun rises through the theatre's globe, stands at `(210, 16)` at noon and sets behind the pub's block; the moon travels from the theatre toward the Radiostacja. These paths are compositional, not astronomical.

## Could not be verified

- **Metrorower on the Rynek itself.** The 29 launch stations listed by the city include none on the Rynek. The rider is passing through; no station is drawn. The later station list was not checked.
- **Aviation lights on the Radiostacja.** Sources describe the tower's floodlights and its LED illumination (since 2009) but none found confirms red obstruction lights today. The three blinking beacons already in the scene were left as they were.
- **Pigeons today.** The photographs are from 2017. The fountain photograph is a winter one; the scene shows summer.
- **Plane trees in 2026.** The photographs show the trees when planted in 2022; their present size was not checked.
- No visit and no photographs from November 2026.
